# TomiTrack 0.4.17 · Early access

Add 466 reviewed item-price listings across 350 additional references: 295 TLV and 55 mainline Tomica. The watch pool is now 511 listings across 364 references. 26 models have qualifying independent listing averages (previously nine). The owner-approved MSRP fallback remains when usable comparables are insufficient.

Market Price uses the item price only, excluding shipping, tax and checkout totals. Exact model/variant and original boxed/unopened condition checks, one comparable per seller and outlier screening remain in place. Market Price and MSRP controls keep the approved layout.

The larger pinned listing pool refreshes with at most three concurrent public requests. Failed sources retain their original dated evidence; ended checks never reset price age or infer a sale. No new marketplace credentials or collection uploads are introduced.

The catalog still contains 17,920 references, 4,268 English Tomica-family entries, 2,450 photos, 2,934 MSRP records and three sparse verified sales. APK, Windows installer, SQLite and checksum manifest are included. Android package/signing identity is unchanged; versionCode 23.
