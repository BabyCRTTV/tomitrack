Privacy notice · Version 0.1.0 · October 16, 2026

What stays on your device

Your model records, notes, prices, wishlist and uploaded photos are saved in local app storage (IndexedDB). TomiTrack 0.1.0 has no accounts, advertising, analytics, cloud collection storage or automatic syncing. Demo data is temporary and separate from your saved collection.

Photos and files

You choose individual photos or backup files using your device’s file picker. Photos are resized before being saved locally. Exported JSON backups include your collection and photos; CSV exports include text records only. Anyone with an exported backup can read it, so keep it somewhere you trust. Importing a backup replaces the current collection after confirmation.

Connections

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub receives ordinary requests to serve the site and may process request information under its privacy policy. The download page requests public release metadata from the GitHub API. Opening external links contacts the destination website. The installed apps bundle the tracker and do not need a connection for collection management.

Removing your data

You can remove individual models from their detail view. Clearing the app’s storage, removing browser site data, or uninstalling the Android app deletes the local collection. Windows uninstallers may retain application data for future updates. Export a backup first if you want to keep it.

Early access

Collection values are manual estimates. No live market data is provided. The starter catalog and car illustrations demonstrate the workflow and do not establish exact releases or variants. Future account, pricing or social features will need a revised privacy notice before introduction.
